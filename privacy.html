Privacy Policy

Storyfluff for Apple TV, by Feather and Fluff. Last updated October 19, 2026.

Storyfluff does not collect any data. We don't run accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and the app has no third-party code that does.

What happens when you play

  • Names and words. The nicknames and words players type live only in your Apple TV's memory for the current game. Phones send them to the Apple TV over your home Wi-Fi, never over the internet, and they're gone when the game ends.
  • Joining from a phone. The phone page is served by your Apple TV itself. It stores a random ID in the phone's browser so a phone can rejoin the same seat after a dropped connection. That ID never leaves your home network.
  • Story Cards. Story Card videos and pictures are made on the Apple TV, offered to phones in the room over your home network, and deleted when the game ends.
  • Narration. The story is read aloud by the Apple TV's built-in voices, on the device. Nothing is sent to a speech or AI service.
  • Settings. Your settings (narrator, rounds, family filter and so on) are saved on the Apple TV only.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account information. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Local network access

Storyfluff asks for local network access so phones in the same room can join the game. It uses this only to talk to phones on your home network.

Children

Storyfluff doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. A family filter, on by default, replaces typed words that match a list of rude words.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If a future version ever collects data, it will say so here and in the App Store listing before it does.
