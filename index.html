Storyfluff Support

Party stories for your couch, on Apple TV. By Feather and Fluff.

Need help? Email featherfluffllc@gmail.com. Tell us your Apple TV model and tvOS version, and what you saw. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

My phone can't join the game.
The phone and the Apple TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Guest networks and some mesh or office networks block devices from seeing each other; use your main network. Turn off any VPN on the phone or the Apple TV. If tvOS asked for local network access and you chose Don't Allow, turn it back on in the Apple TV's Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Storyfluff.
Can I play without phones?
Yes. Choose Play with the Remote. You can type with the on-screen keyboard, hold the Siri Remote's mic button to dictate, or pass the remote around.
I bought packs. How do I get them on another Apple TV?
Open Packs (or Settings) and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account. Purchases can also be shared with your Family Sharing group.
What does Unlock Everything include?
Every pack available today and every pack we add later, for one price. Mature packs, if any are added, appear only when Settings → Show Mature Packs is turned on.
Is Prep & Petty for kids?
No. Prep & Petty is set at a fictional university, finishing academy and members-only club, with an all-adult cast in adult settings. Its humor is about manners, status and exes, with light innuendo at most. Parents can hide it in Settings → Show Adult-Themed Packs.
What is PG Mode, and what if I forget its PIN?
PG Mode (Settings → PG Mode) is for family rooms: it hides Prep & Petty, uses gentler story text where there is some, and filters more words. You can lock it with a 4-digit parent PIN. After 5 wrong tries, PIN entry pauses for 1 minute, and each further wrong try doubles the pause. If you forget the PIN, choose Turn Off PG Mode → Forgot PIN? and answer the grown-up multiplication question; that clears the PIN. PG Mode and its PIN are kept on your Apple TV even if the app is deleted and reinstalled, and they never leave the device.
How do I save a Story Card?
After a round, choose Make a Story Card on the TV. Phones in the game get a Save button, or you can scan the QR code on the TV. Open the download and choose Save to Photos.
Who is the narrator?
Narration voices are AI-generated (Deepgram). Pick one of four narrators in Settings → Narrator: Draco, Aries, Cordelia or Vesta. Their lines are made ahead of time from our own stories; nothing you type is sent anywhere. Players' words are read out by your Apple TV's own voice, right after a little "answer" sound, and only if they pass the family filter.
Does Storyfluff collect my data?
No. See the privacy policy.
